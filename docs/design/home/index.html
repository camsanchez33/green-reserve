Questions

What courses ask us.

What is GreenReserve?

An online booking page and tee sheet for golf courses. Golfers book online; your staff run the day from one sheet.

Does it work with our website?

Yes. You add a “Book a tee time” button that opens your booking page.

Can we still take phone and walk-in bookings?

Yes. They go on the same tee sheet, so online golfers only see what's really open.

How do we get paid?

Green fees go to your course's own Stripe account.

Do members get their own rates?

Yes. Member rates and booking windows are set by tier.

What does it cost?

Nothing to your course. Book a demo and we'll walk you through it.
