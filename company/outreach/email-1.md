# Email 1 — first touch

**To:** the course's published business contact (pro shop, manager, or the parks/recreation office for
a town or county course). **From:** Cam, admin@greenreserve.app. Plain text, no images, no tracking
pixels, no attachments — a first email from a stranger with any of those lands in spam.

Fill the `{…}` from the list. If you can't fill `{one true thing}` with something real you saw on their
website, leave the sentence out — a fake personal line is worse than none.

---

**Subject:** Online tee times for {Course name}

Hi {first name, or "there" if no name is published},

I'm Cam, the founder of GreenReserve. We built an online booking page and tee sheet for public golf
courses, and I'm looking for a few courses in the Hudson Valley to set up first.

{One true thing — e.g. "I saw that tee times at {Course} are booked by phone." / "I saw {Course}
offers a resident rate."}

What your course would get: its own booking page in your name, logo and colors; one tee sheet for
online, phone and walk-in bookings; and golfers who pay with one card payment to your course's own
Stripe account.

No setup fee, no monthly fee, no commission on your green fees. Golfers pay $1.50 per player on each
online booking, added to your price.

I set every course up myself. If it's worth a look, you can pick a time for a demo here:
https://greenreserve.app/demo

Or just reply and tell me how you take bookings today.

Cam
Founder, GreenReserve
admin@greenreserve.app · (845) 499-3515
greenreserve.app

---

## Why it's written this way

- **The ask is one line with a choice.** The task asks for one ask; "pick a time, or reply" is one ask
  with two doors, because a GM who isn't ready for a demo will still answer a question.
- **The fee is in the first email.** "Free" without the $1.50 reads like a trick, and "how do you make
  money" is the first thing they'd ask. Both sentences are LQ-2's, word for word.
- **No feature list.** Three things a counter cares about. The rest is for the demo.
- **"A few courses … first" is true** (there are no live courses yet) and it is the reason to answer now.
  Don't claim other courses are using it until one is.
