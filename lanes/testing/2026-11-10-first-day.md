Walked locally against `main` at `cb7a92c` (#352), throwaway Postgres, `next dev`, seed course `perf-audit` plus evening tee times for today, four shop items, an owner login and a **Front desk** preset staff login (`frontdesk@ci.local`). Laptop 1440 and phone 390. No Stripe, Twilio or Resend keys in this environment, so every payment was cash (see Not tested).

The day, as walked: owner opens the register ($200) → owner adds a cash walk-in (Wally, 2 + cart, $170) → Front desk adds a phone booking (Pat, 4) → golfer books online on a phone (Olive, 3, quoted $199.50) → Front desk checks Pat in 3 of 4 with cash ($195) → Front desk checks Olive in 1 of 3 (cash $65), then the other 2 (cash $130) → Front desk sells a cap + 2 waters at the register ($34, $50 given, $16 change) → Front desk closes out (counted $794, even, left $200, deposit $594) → Front desk marks Nora (6:00, 2 players) a no-show → owner reads Home and Money.

## Must fix

### M1. Front desk takes cash on the tee sheet without ever seeing the amount
- **Steps:** as Front desk, open Pat Phone's 7:20 group → Check in → 3 of 4 → Next → Next → Payment.
- **What happened:** the Payment step shows "How are they paying? · 3 players", a **Take cash** button and nothing else — no total, no Exact / $50 / $100 / Other, no change due. The amount is never shown at any step of the check-in. The register, by contrast, shows Front desk "Take $34.00 cash" and the change, and Close out shows them the whole day's cash ($594).
- **What a course would say:** "My counter person has to ask the golfer what they owe." This is the main job of the Front desk preset (it carries `sheet.counter_payment`), and it can't be done without a second login.
- **Evidence:** `staff-checkin-step3.png`. `src/app/dashboard/page.tsx:926` `showMoney = access.can('money.payments')`, and every amount on the payment step is behind `showMoney` (`page.tsx:1104` total, `:1112` "Exact" vs "Take cash", `:1113–1114` the quick-cash and Other buttons). Front desk preset `src/lib/staff-permissions.ts:95` has no `money.payments`.
- **Smallest fix:** show the amount due (and the quick-cash/change buttons) whenever the login can take the payment (`sheet.counter_payment`), and keep `money.payments` for the reports. Any other fix (adding `money.payments` to Front desk) also opens Money's reports to them — Cam's call (Q1).

### M2. Money gives three different answers to "Not paid yet" on one page
- **Steps:** as owner after the day above, open Money.
- **What happened:** the top strip says **Not paid yet · 7 days $195 · 2 groups**; "How it was paid" right below says **Not paid yet · 0 $0.00**; the Payments filter says **Not paid yet 1**. Home agrees with the top strip ($195, 2 groups). The $195 is Pat's guest ($65, truly owed, plays at 7:20) plus Nora's no-show ($130, no card on file — it will never be paid).
- **What a course would say:** "Which one is it?" — and "$195 owed" is wrong for the owner reading it at 6pm: $130 of it is a no-show with no card to charge.
- **Evidence:** `owner-money-1440.png`, `owner-money-390.png`, `owner-home-1440.png`. Top strip `src/components/dashboard/money/SalesPanel.tsx:144`; the 0 row `SalesPanel.tsx:230` (`s.paid.outstandingGroups`); the filter `src/components/dashboard/money/PaymentsPanel.tsx:34–44` (no-shows are their own filter there, so it counts 1).
- **Smallest fix:** one definition, used by all three and by Home. Recommend: unpaid = confirmed, not a no-show, tee time today or earlier, money still due (no-shows shown on their own line, with the fee only when there's a card to charge). Q2.

## What's good

- Cash is airtight: every cash payment became a register sale and a ledger row, and the close-out's expected $794 matched to the cent with nothing typed by hand.
- Partial check-in is smooth: "How many are here? 3 of 4 · The other 1 stays booked" and the rest shows under the group, ready for its own check-in. Paying separately (1 of 3, then 2) took two short passes and the shares added up ($65 + $130).
- The closed register really is closed: tee-sheet cash is refused with a clear message (409 `registerClosed`), and on a laptop the notice offers "Open the register" from Home and the sheet.
- The blind count ("Count first; the day's cash shows after") then Even / deposit / float / note is exactly how a drawer should close.
- The golfer's phone booking flow: four taps from course page to "You're all set", with a clear "charged today $0.00", and the SMS consent box unticked. The 7:00 time with 2 spots was hidden for a party of 3.
- Mark no-show only appears 10 minutes after the time, and "Still coming" undoes it.
- No horizontal scroll at 390 on the tee sheet, Pro shop, Home or Money.
