Same pages, same layout. Only backgrounds, panels and buttons change.

Every picture is a screenshot of the real app running on example data. Pick a style, then comment by tab and number, for example "Dashboard 1, light". After the screenshots, each tab shows the new phone design for every page, with a few lines on what is different from today. Only the phone layout changes; the computer stays exactly as shown, and nothing is added or removed.

Style

Dark is glass the way Apple does it: a clean flat dark page, and panels made of frosted material (a translucent dark fill that blurs and saturates whatever sits behind it, a faint white hairline, a soft inner top highlight and a soft shadow). No shapes, no gradient, no colour cast. Light is solid: white cards on a neutral grey page, with the dark sidebar. There is no search in the sidebar; every page search matches its screen (white on Light, dark glass on Dark). The sidebars have small group labels, one line icon per item and a clearly marked current page. Admin is dark only. The homepage shows its current look and Light (solid). Status colours keep their meaning in every style: green paid, amber owes or soon, red a problem.
