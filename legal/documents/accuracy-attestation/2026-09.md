## 1. What you are confirming

You confirm that the information you have given GreenReserve about your course — its name, address, holes, tee time schedule, green fees, cart fees, member and resident rates, cancellation and no-show policy, and the details on your setup sheet — is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge as of the date you sign.

## 2. Keeping it current

You agree to update your prices, schedule and policies in your GreenReserve dashboard when they change, before golfers book against the old ones. GreenReserve shows golfers what you have entered; it does not check it against any other source.

## 3. If something is wrong

If a golfer books at a price or under a policy that was wrong because your information was out of date, you will honour that booking as made or resolve it directly with the golfer. GreenReserve is not responsible for losses that come from inaccurate information you supplied.

## 4. Authority

By signing, you confirm you are authorised to make these statements on behalf of the course.
