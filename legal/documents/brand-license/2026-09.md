This license covers how GreenReserve may use your course's name, logo and photographs ("your brand assets").

## 1. Your booking page

You grant GreenReserve a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to display your brand assets on the booking page GreenReserve operates for your course, in the emails and receipts GreenReserve sends to your golfers about your course, and in the tee sheet and dashboard your staff use. This use lasts for as long as your course is listed on GreenReserve.

## 2. GreenReserve's marketing

Unless you opt out, you also grant GreenReserve a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to show your course name and a screenshot of your booking page in GreenReserve's own marketing — for example on the GreenReserve homepage, in a case study, or in material shown to other courses. You may opt out of this marketing use at any time by email to hello@greenreserve.app or in your dashboard settings; GreenReserve will stop new uses within 10 business days and will not create new material using your brand after that.

## 3. What GreenReserve will not do

GreenReserve will not alter your logo beyond resizing and cropping for layout, will not use your brand assets to promote another course, and will not sell or sublicense them to anyone.

## 4. Your promise about the assets

You confirm that you own, or have permission to use and license, every logo and photograph you upload, and that their use as described here does not infringe anyone else's rights. If a photograph is later found to be one you were not entitled to license, you will tell GreenReserve and GreenReserve will remove it.

## 5. Ending the license

When your course is removed from GreenReserve, the booking-page license ends and your assets are removed from your page within 24 hours. GreenReserve may keep marketing material created while the license was in force unless you have opted out, in which case it is withdrawn within 10 business days.
